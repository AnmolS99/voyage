# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

voyage is an app for exploring a 3D globe and keeping track of the countries
you have visited and want to visit. It is made by Anmol Singh, an independent
developer. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use voyage
on iOS or Android.

**In short: voyage has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your
travel data stays on your device and in your own iCloud or Google backup — the
developer never receives it.**

## What voyage stores

voyage saves the things you enter so it can show them back to you:

- the countries you have marked as visited or on your wishlist
- the cities and attractions you have checked off
- your settings (theme, globe style, view mode and similar)
- your progress in challenges and the daily quiz

This data is stored on your device only. It is never sent to the developer or
to any server operated by the developer.

### iOS: iCloud sync

On iOS, voyage also keeps a copy of your saved data in your iCloud key-value
storage, so it stays in sync across your devices signed in to the same Apple
Account. That copy is stored by Apple under your account and is covered by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer
cannot access it. You can turn this off for voyage in your device's iCloud
settings.

### Android: device backup

On Android, your saved data is included in Android's standard backup and
device-to-device transfer, if you have backup turned on. Those backups are
handled by Google under your Google Account and covered by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer
cannot access them.

## Network access

The Android app makes no network connections.

The iOS app connects to the internet for the Daily Challenge only: it downloads
each day's question from a database hosted by
[Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy). These requests contain no personal
data or identifiers. Like any web request, they reveal your device's IP
address to Supabase, which may keep it in short-lived server logs. Your answers
are not sent anywhere — they are saved only on your device.

## Tips (in-app purchases)

The iOS app has an optional tip jar. Tips are processed entirely by Apple
through the App Store. voyage never sees your payment details. Apple tells the
app only whether a purchase succeeded.

## What voyage does not do

- no account or sign-in
- no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs
- no tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifiers
- no access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
- no selling or sharing of personal data

## Children

voyage is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data
from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device and in your own backups, you
control it entirely. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. On iOS,
you can stop the iCloud copy syncing by turning off iCloud for voyage. On Android, you can manage backups in your Google
Account settings.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you rights to access, correct
and delete your personal data. The developer holds no personal data about you,
so in practice the data that exists is the data on your devices and in your own
cloud accounts, described above. You can still contact the developer with any
question or request.
