Every country now has its own painting: open a country and its card shows hand-painted art of the place, by day and by night.

- Daylight theme: the app turns light at sunrise and dark at sunset
- A dark app icon, chosen automatically or in Settings
- Filter the country list to show only places you've visited or want to visit
- Zoom the map with one finger: tap, then press and drag
- Tap Home again to turn the globe back to where it started
- Ticking off a city or sight now marks its country visited
- Challenge answers stay up longer, and a tap or swipe dismisses them
- Press Enter to take the top suggestion when naming countries and capitals
- Countries with more than one capital now accept them all
- Top cities refreshed to favour the places travellers actually go
- Removing countries or resetting now syncs across your devices
- A smoother map, and less memory used after launch
